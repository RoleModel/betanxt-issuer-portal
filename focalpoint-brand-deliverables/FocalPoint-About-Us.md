# About FocalPoint

FocalPoint is a national strategic communications and media relations firm. We help corporate, public sector, and nonprofit clients tell their stories, manage their reputations, and navigate the moments that matter most, from product launches to policy fights to crises that unfold in real time.

What started as a single regional communications practice has grown into one of the largest independent strategic communications firms in the country. Today, more than 6,800 people work across 28 offices nationwide, supporting more than 1,200 active client relationships.

## What We Do

FocalPoint brings together four connected disciplines under one roof:

- **Media Relations & Public Relations** — earned media strategy, press outreach, and reputation management for organizations of every size.
- **Public Affairs & Government Relations** — advocacy, coalition building, and policy communications for clients navigating regulatory and legislative landscapes.
- **Digital & Creative Services** — content, design, and campaign work built for the channels where audiences actually pay attention.
- **Crisis & Issues Management** — rapid-response counsel and communications support when the stakes are highest.

## How We Work

Our clients come to us because the lines between these disciplines have blurred. A product recall is a crisis story, a policy story, and a digital story all at once. We staff our teams to move across that boundary instead of getting stuck on one side of it.

## Beyond the Work

We hold ourselves to the same standard of integrity we bring to every campaign. The FocalPoint Foundation provides pro bono communications support and media literacy programming in the communities where we operate, and our internal commitments around data privacy, responsible use of AI in content production, and workforce diversity are detailed each year in our Corporate Responsibility Report.
